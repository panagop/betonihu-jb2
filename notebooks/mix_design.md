This section covers the basics of concrete mix design, which is a subsection of the materials chapter.

## Water-Cement Ratio

The water-cement ratio ($w/c$) is the most important factor affecting concrete strength [@neville2011]. A lower $w/c$ ratio leads to higher strength, but reduces workability.

Typical $w/c$ ratios for structural concrete range from 0.40 to 0.55 [@en206].

## Aggregate Selection

The maximum aggregate size and grading influence the workability and strength of concrete. According to @neville2011, the aggregate should be well-graded to minimize voids and cement paste requirements.
