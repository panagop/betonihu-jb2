# Bending Design

This chapter introduces the design of reinforced concrete sections subjected to bending according to Eurocode 2 [@en1992].

## Assumptions

The following assumptions are made for the ultimate limit state design of bending [@wight2016]:

1. Plane sections remain plane after bending
2. The tensile strength of concrete is neglected
3. The stress-strain relationship of concrete follows a parabolic-rectangular diagram
4. The maximum compressive strain in concrete is $\varepsilon_{cu} = 0.0035$

## Rectangular Stress Block

For design purposes, a simplified rectangular stress block can be used [@mosley2012]. The depth of the stress block is:

$$
s = \lambda \cdot x
$$

where $\lambda = 0.8$ for concrete classes up to C50/60, and $x$ is the neutral axis depth.
