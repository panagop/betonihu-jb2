# Materials

This chapter covers the material properties used in reinforced concrete design according to Eurocode 2 [@en1992].

## Concrete

Concrete is classified by its characteristic compressive cylinder strength $f_{ck}$, measured on cylinders at 28 days. The most commonly used concrete classes in building construction are C20/25 through C50/60 [@wight2016].

The design compressive strength is given by:

$$
f_{cd} = \frac{f_{ck}}{\gamma_c}
$$

where $\gamma_c = 1.5$ is the partial safety factor for concrete.

## Reinforcing Steel

The standard reinforcing steel grade used in Europe is B500, with a characteristic yield strength $f_{yk} = 500$ MPa [@en1992]. The design yield strength is:

$$
f_{yd} = \frac{f_{yk}}{\gamma_s}
$$

where $\gamma_s = 1.15$ is the partial safety factor for steel [@mosley2012].
